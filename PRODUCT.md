# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily men in the United States who follow large lifestyle and health/longevity influencers, discovering REALITY.EXE through Instagram, YouTube Shorts, or other creator platforms and opening the site directly on a phone. They are performance- and self-optimization-minded, comfortable with premium technology purchases, and arrive with some trust already transferred from the influencer, but no prior brand awareness. The job to be done: decide, within seconds, whether this is a serious technology brand worth their attention, then evaluate and purchase a specific device.

## Product Purpose

REALITY.EXE is a premium wellness/longevity technology brand launching with portable red-light therapy devices (head/scalp, facial/mask, and wearable body form factors) sold directly through this site. The site's job is to establish REALITY.EXE as a distinctive, culturally relevant technology brand — not a red-light-therapy store — while still functioning as a fully working, conversion-focused ecommerce storefront. Success is a visitor who is intrigued within five seconds, stays for the story, and completes a purchase understanding exactly what they're buying and why it's credible.

## Positioning

The default settings of your body and environment are not mandatory — REALITY.EXE builds the technology to question and override them, starting with light.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: purchase a device (add to cart / buy).
- Secondary CTA: explore the technology/science before buying (scroll into the credibility/specs layer, or view a product's full technical file).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "The default is not optimal" / "REALITY.EXE — exit the default."
- Belief ladder: (1) this is not a generic wellness brand, something else is going on here → (2) the mystery resolves into real, serious technology, not conspiracy aesthetics → (3) the science and specs are legitimate and well-documented → (4) the specific device solves a need I already have → (5) I trust this enough to buy now.
- Proof on hand: none yet (pre-launch). Product specs, certifications, and testimonials are placeholders in this first build and are explicitly marked as such rather than fabricated as real claims.

## Brand Personality

Intelligent, minimal, slightly provocative, confident, controlled, mysterious. Speaks in short, deliberate statements — commands, observations, occasionally information the visitor feels they weren't quite supposed to see. Never childish, never aggressive, never fake-scientific. Luxury technology and dystopian editorial design rather than hacker/cyberpunk/conspiracy cliché.

## Anti-references

Generic Shopify/wellness/SaaS ecommerce templates. Literal Matrix rain, green terminal text, Illuminati eyes, pyramids, aliens, hacker clichés, constant glitch effects, cyberpunk visual overload. Tiny uppercase tracked "eyebrow" labels and 01/02/03 section numbering used as reflexive scaffolding rather than earned sequence. Typical wellness copy ("unlock your potential," "transform your life") and corporate SaaS language. Cheap conspiracy-merch tone.

## Design Principles

- Mystery must resolve into credibility — every experimental or cryptic moment on the homepage is followed, eventually, by a calmer, technically serious moment that earns trust.
- One dominant idea per screen, especially on mobile — the homepage reads as a vertical film, not a dense marketing page.
- Motion narrates, it doesn't decorate — the black-to-white "system opening" transition and other major animations exist to advance the DEFAULT REALITY → INTERRUPTION → DISCOVERY → SYSTEM OPENING → LIGHT → DEVICE → EVIDENCE → PURCHASE sequence, not to show off.
- Commerce is never sacrificed to art direction — navigation, product understanding, and purchase actions stay unambiguous even inside the most experimental sections.
- Red is a signal, not a decoration — it marks active/energized/scanning states against a black-and-white field, it does not wash over everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout (crossfade/instant alternatives for every scroll-driven and pinned animation, including the black-doors-opening moment). Maintain WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text, ≥3:1 large text) even against the black and white "drenched" surfaces. Keep all commerce flows (navigation, product info, cart, checkout stub) fully usable without relying on the cinematic motion layer.
